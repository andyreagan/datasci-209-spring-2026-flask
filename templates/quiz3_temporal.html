Key Concepts from This Week

Connection marks emphasize ordering - they show the relationship between one item and the next.
Granularity: year/month/day vs. year/month/day/hour. Truncate or use dateparts for aggregation.
Aspect ratio matters: "Banking to 45 degrees" - slopes near 45 degrees are most accurately perceived.
Indexed line charts show change over time using a normalized baseline (e.g., 100 = start).
Radial layouts appeal to cyclical time (hours, seasons) but sacrifice precision.
Horizon charts compress vertical space by layering bands - great for many series.
